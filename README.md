## How to write the name

**Oxlume**: one word, capital O, the rest lowercase. Not OXLUME, OxLume or Ox Lume. The name joins "ox", the animal that does the steady work, with "lume", light.

## Please

- Use the files as they are. Do not change the colours, proportions or shape of the mark, add effects or redraw the logo.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the mark's centre bar.
- Use `logo-on-dark.svg` on dark or photo backgrounds and `logo.svg` on light ones.
